Hemline categories describe where the skirt ends relative to the body. A worn skirt requires its wearer's visible reference and a matching calibration.

Hanging and held skirts use a calibrated standard body reference and are labelled as estimates. Physical length in centimetres is not available.

Research station. Predictions can be unavailable; fabric appearance and calibrated length are not yet validated for demonstration use.

Framing and setup guidance

Stand facing the camera with one skirt fully visible. Include the waist, hem, feet and floor for length assessment; your face does not need to be in view. Use even lighting and a clear background.
